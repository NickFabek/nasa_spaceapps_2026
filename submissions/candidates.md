# Variable-star candidates found with Spherical

Fourteen stars flagged by the scan, ordered by NEOWISE amplitude. For every one:

- SPHEREx shows the change at matched wavelengths, with a stable position, agreement with the 2010 WISE
  position, and flat neighbouring stars;
- ten years of NEOWISE single-exposure photometry independently show it varies (errors per visit are
  0.003–0.013 mag);
- it is not listed as variable in SIMBAD, the VizieR copy of the AAVSO VSX, Gaia DR3 (flag and variability
  tables), ZTF periodic (Chen+ 2020), WISE periodic (Chen+ 2018), WISE variables (Petrosky+ 2021), the AGB
  catalogue of Suh (2021) or the extreme-AGB list of Groenewegen (2022).

None is confirmed by anyone else, and the live VSX index was not checked (see `README.md` in this folder).
Red stars in the Galactic plane are commonly variable and optical surveys miss them, so finds like these are
expected to be plentiful; they are new to the catalogues, not rare objects. Drafts for the first three found
are in this folder.
