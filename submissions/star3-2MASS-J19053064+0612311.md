## Remarks to paste

Extremely red source (2MASS J−Ks = 5.45, Ks = 8.58) with no Gaia DR3 counterpart, at Galactic l = 40°, b = −0.3°;
probably a dust-enshrouded giant.
NEOWISE-R photometry (272 exposures, 20 visits, 2014–2024) shows variation of 0.36 mag in W1 and 0.27 mag in W2,
with maxima about every two years; visits are six months apart, so no period is claimed.
SPHEREx spectrophotometry (DR1 and QR3) shows steady brightening through three passes: at 4.7 µm the median flux
was 187, 209 and 231 mJy in Sep 2025, Apr 2026 and Sep 2026 (about W2 = 7.41 to 7.18); at matched wavelengths the
rise over the year was 24% at 4.7 µm, 21% at 4.1 µm and 22% at 2 µm.
Not listed in SIMBAD; not in the VizieR copies of VSX, ZTF periodic (Chen+ 2020), WISE periodic (Chen+ 2018) or
WISE variables (Petrosky+ 2021). AllWISE var_flag = 0111.

## Honest weaknesses

The two-year cycle rests on about four cycles sampled twice a year. The field is crowded; the 2MASS and WISE
positions agree to 0.2″ and SPHEREx sees a single stable source.
