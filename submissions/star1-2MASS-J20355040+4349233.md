## Remarks to paste

Very red source (2MASS J−Ks = 4.16, Ks = 9.32) with no Gaia DR3 counterpart, at Galactic l = 82.5°, b = +1.8°.
NEOWISE-R single-exposure photometry (434 exposures, 21 visits, 2014–2024) shows irregular variation of 0.22 mag in
W1 and 0.21 mag in W2 on timescales of one to two years; the median error per visit is 0.003 mag.
SPHEREx spectrophotometry (DR1 and QR3) compared at matched wavelengths shows fading of 13–24% in five bands between
1.1 and 5.0 µm from Jun 2025 to Jun 2026, most of it by Oct 2025, while twelve field stars stayed constant within 3%.
Not listed in SIMBAD; not in the VizieR copies of VSX, the ZTF periodic catalogue (Chen+ 2020), the WISE periodic
catalogue (Chen+ 2018) or the WISE variables catalogue (Petrosky+ 2021). AllWISE var_flag = 000n.
