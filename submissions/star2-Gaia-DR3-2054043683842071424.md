## Remarks to paste

Hα-excess source in Fratta et al. 2021 (MNRAS 505, 1135); G = 14.3, BP−RP = 2.6.
NEOWISE-R photometry (364 exposures, 21 visits, 2014–2024) shows a decline from W1 = 9.51 in 2014.4 to a quiescent
level of W1 = 10.2 held from 2017 to mid-2020, followed by recurrent infrared brightenings of 0.4 mag in W1 and
0.6 mag in W2 at 2020.8, 2022.4 and 2023.8–2024.4. The W1−W2 colour is redder in outburst.
SPHEREx spectrophotometry (DR1 and QR3) shows a further episode: at matched wavelengths the flux rose from May 2025
to May 2026 by 23% at 1.4 µm, 32% at 2 µm, 59% at 4.1 µm and 77% at 4.7 µm, with no change below 1.1 µm.
May 2025 fluxes correspond to about W1 = 10.13, May 2026 to about W1 = 9.44.
ZTF g and r photometry is constant within 0.05 mag from 2018 to Oct 2025 (g = 16.57, r = 14.53).
The behaviour (Hα emission, infrared excess that builds and decays, little optical change) is consistent with a Be
star whose circumstellar disk forms and dissipates. Not flagged variable in Gaia DR3; not in the VizieR copies of
VSX, ZTF periodic (Chen+ 2020), WISE periodic (Chen+ 2018) or WISE variables (Petrosky+ 2021).

## Honest weaknesses

The Be-star interpretation is an inference from photometry; a young star with a variable inner disk would look
similar. The colon after the type says so.
