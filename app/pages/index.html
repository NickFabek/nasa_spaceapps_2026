Find what moves.

NASA's SPHEREx telescope re-photographs the entire sky every six months, in 102 infrared colours. Stars stay put. Nearby things — asteroids, comets, brown dwarfs, perhaps an undiscovered planet — shift between passes.

Most known brown dwarfs were found by volunteers doing exactly this with an older telescope. SPHEREx is new, and almost none of its sky has been looked at by a person. Pick any patch.
